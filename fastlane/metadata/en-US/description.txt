Seven tiny people live in a town in your pocket. They rob each other, start businesses, bill the room, declare enemies for no reason, run for office, print money and go to jail. Nobody is in charge. You are barely in charge.

WHAT HAPPENS
Every couple of seconds, somebody does something. A being with no money steals. A being with no friends starts giving coins away. Whoever holds the Crown prints money, and everything in town gets dearer. Somebody founds a gang. Somebody founds a book club. Somebody is exiled by vote and a stranger moves into the empty house. It all lands in the town newspaper, The Daily Grudge, and in the yard where they wander about and bump into each other.

MEDDLE
Tap anyone to open their file, then give them 50 coins, rob them, jail them, crown them or exile them. The town remembers: gifts make friends, robberies make grudges.

A WISH EVERY DAY
Each day the town has a wish for you: get Nan into a gang, make Gary the richest being in town, push prices past ×2. Make it happen any way you like. Granted wishes earn a miracle and grow your streak.

MIRACLES
Drop an act of the sky on the town: a Coup that empties every seat, a Snap Election, a Plague of Frogs, a Gold Rush, a Festival, a Twister that swaps every wallet, a Haunting that changes every face. One free miracle every day.

INVITE ANYONE
Name a stranger, pick a face, and they move in with 100 coins and no friends. Your friends, your boss, your cat. The town grows to ten.

LAWS
Pass up to three: No Gambling, Tough on Crime, Free Market, Term Limits, Basic Coin, Martial Law. The town lives by them until you repeal them. (With The Big Hand.)

THE HALL OF RECORDS
Richest ever, biggest heist, most wanted, crowned most, arrests, exiles and more, kept for as long as the town lasts.

YARDS AND WIDGETS
Play on the classic lawn or pick a yard: Snow Day, Night Market, Beach Town or Mars Colony, each with its own app icon. The Daily Grudge widget puts your town's latest scandal on your Home Screen.

COME BACK LATER
Close the app and the town keeps going. Open it again and the paper tells you what you missed.

No accounts, no sign-up, no internet connection, nothing collected about you. Every being is a made-up cartoon character; no real person, company, place or event appears in this game.
