Seven tiny people live in a town in your pocket. They rob each other, run for office, print money and go to jail. Nobody is in charge. You are barely in charge.

WHAT HAPPENS
Every few seconds, trouble: the broke steal, the lonely hand out coins, whoever holds the Crown prints money and prices climb. Gangs form, someone is voted out, a stranger moves in. It all lands in The Daily Grudge, the town paper.

MEDDLE
Tap anyone to give them 50 coins, rob them, jail them, crown them or exile them. The town remembers: gifts make friends, robberies make grudges.

A GIFT AND A WISH EVERY DAY
Open the daily gift and walk a 7-day calendar of miracles and invitations; day seven is the big box. Then grant today's wish (get Nan into a gang, push prices past ×2) for a miracle and a longer streak.

A NEW SEASON EVERY MONTH
Every month brings a Season with a 10-tier track: Spooky Season, Election Season, Holiday Heist and more. Earn stars from gifts, wishes, three weekly challenges and new stamps, claim a reward on every tier and keep the Season's face.

THE STAMP ALBUM
24 stamps to earn from what your towns get up to, from First Heist to Wheelbarrow Economy.

MIRACLES
Drop an act of the sky on the town: a Coup, a Snap Election, a Plague of Frogs, a Twister that swaps every wallet and more.

INVITE ANYONE
Name a stranger, pick a face, and they move in with 100 coins and no friends. The town grows to ten.

LAWS
Pass up to three, from No Gambling to Martial Law, and the town lives by them until you repeal them.

YOUR TOWN ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
Town Square shows the whole yard and who holds the Crown; The Daily Grudge carries the latest scandal. Plus Lock Screen widgets, a Control Center button and "Town Report" for Siri and Shortcuts.

YARDS AND RECORDS
Swap the classic lawn for Snow Day, Night Market, Beach Town or Mars Colony, each with its own app icon. The Hall of Records keeps the richest ever and the biggest heist.
