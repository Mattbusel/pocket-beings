Seven tiny people live in a town in your pocket. They rob each other, start businesses, bill the room, declare enemies for no reason, run for office, print money and go to jail. Nobody is in charge. You are barely in charge.

WHAT HAPPENS
Every couple of seconds, somebody does something. A being with no money steals. A being with no friends starts giving coins away. Whoever holds the Crown prints money, and everything in town gets dearer. Somebody founds a gang. Somebody founds a book club. Somebody is exiled by vote and a stranger moves into the empty house. It all lands in the town newspaper, The Daily Grudge, and in the yard where they wander about and bump into each other.

MEDDLE
Tap anyone to open their file, then give them 50 coins, rob them, jail them, crown them or exile them. The town remembers: gifts make friends, robberies make grudges.

A GIFT AND A WISH EVERY DAY
Open the daily gift and walk along a 7-day calendar of miracles and invitations; day seven is the big box. Then make today's wish come true: get Nan into a gang, make Gary the richest being in town, push prices past ×2. Granted wishes earn a miracle and grow your streak.

A NEW SEASON EVERY MONTH
Every month is a Season with its own name and a 10-tier track: Spooky Season, Election Season, Holiday Heist and more. Earn stars from gifts, wishes, three weekly challenges and new stamps, claim rewards on every tier, and finish the Season to keep its face for your invitations.

THE STAMP ALBUM
24 stamps to collect from what happens in your towns: First Heist, Dynasty, Funny Money, Full Cells, Wheelbarrow Economy, Ancient History and the rest.

MIRACLES
Drop an act of the sky on the town: a Coup that empties every seat, a Snap Election, a Plague of Frogs, a Gold Rush, a Festival, a Twister that swaps every wallet, a Haunting that changes every face.

INVITE ANYONE
Name a stranger, pick a face, and they move in with 100 coins and no friends. Your friends, your boss, your cat. The town grows to ten.

LAWS
Pass up to three: No Gambling, Tough on Crime, Free Market, Term Limits, Basic Coin, Martial Law. The town lives by them until you repeal them.

YOUR TOWN ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
Town Square shows the whole yard with everyone in it, who holds the Crown and who is in jail, with a refresh button. The Daily Grudge carries the latest scandal, there are Lock Screen widgets, a Control Center button, and "Town Report" for Siri and Shortcuts.

YARDS AND RECORDS
Play on the classic lawn or pick a yard: Snow Day, Night Market, Beach Town or Mars Colony, each with its own app icon. The Hall of Records keeps the richest ever, the biggest heist, the most wanted and more.
