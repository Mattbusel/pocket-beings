Seven tiny people live in a town in your pocket. They rob each other, start businesses, bill the room, declare enemies for no reason, run for office, print money and go to jail. Nobody is in charge. You are barely in charge.

WHAT HAPPENS

Every couple of seconds, somebody does something. A being with no money steals. A being with no friends starts giving coins away. A being with no title runs for office, and once it holds the Crown it starts printing money, which makes everything in town cost more. Somebody founds a gang. Somebody founds a book club. Somebody is exiled by vote and a stranger moves into the empty house.

It all shows up in the town newspaper, one line at a time, in the yard where they wander about and bump into each other.

MEDDLE

Tap anyone to open their file: coins, business, faction, gang, heat, office, rank. Then ruin their day, or make it:

- Give them 50 coins
- Rob them and hand it to somebody else
- Throw them in jail, no trial
- Crown them
- Exile them

The town remembers. Gifts make friends. Robberies make grudges.

COME BACK LATER

Close the app and the town keeps going. Open it again and the paper tells you what you missed: who took the Sheriff's seat, who lost everything on one bet, who got arrested.

WHAT IT IS NOT

No accounts. No sign-up. No adverts. No in-app purchases. No subscription. No internet connection. Nothing is collected about you. One purchase, one town, forever.

Every being is a made-up cartoon character. No real person, company, place or event appears in this game.
